# Tech Sentry Brand System v1

Source of truth: Google Doc “Tech Sentry — Brand System v1”.

## Core
Operational Intelligence for Technology and Business.

## Product accents
- Tech Sentry: Cyan / Blue
- Sentry-ITSM: Blue / Cyan
- Sentry-OS: Emerald / Cyan
- Sentry-Mobile Home: Violet / Cyan
- Sentry-Nexus: Indigo / Rose
- Sentry-Master: Violet
- Roadmap / development-state only: Amber

## Sentry-Nexus
- Positioning: Community Operations for faith organizations.
- Audience: churches, ministries, fields and congregations.
- Commercial status: in development; do not present as generally available or priced.
- Current technical foundation may be described only when supported by the product repository: organizational hierarchy, people, roles/departments, agenda, public directory/profile, RBAC, link requests, congregation transfers and audit/governance.
- Use Indigo / Rose as the product accent. Amber is reserved for the “Em desenvolvimento” status signal.

## Guardrails
- NOW and ROADMAP must never be visually or verbally conflated.
- Sentry-ITSM, Sentry-OS, Sentry-Mobile Home and Sentry-Nexus are independent product domains.
- Sentry-Nexus must be labeled as in development until commercial readiness is explicitly approved.
- Sentry-Master is the SaaS Control Plane, not a bundled operational product.
- Do not claim Master provisioning/billing for a product until that product's integration is verified.
- Avoid cyber-security stock visual clichés, excessive neon, religious stereotypes or imagery tied to a specific denomination when communicating Sentry-Nexus generically.
